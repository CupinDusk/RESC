# Material scope

Third-party distributed research source is only the public PicoRV32 files
identified by `UPSTREAM_BINDING.json`, under their unchanged ISC `COPYING`
and inline source notice. Their original bytes remain in `upstream/` and in
attempt snapshots. No proprietary overlay, company material or credentials
were used. All workload C/startup/linker, wrappers, drivers, exact-log/audit
orchestration and schemas are this personal research project's own material;
the graph/certificate core is its source-bound earlier R05 own research code.

Zig/Yosys/CXXRTL headers and installed runtimes are external build dependencies
identified by tool commands/versions, not redistributed. Host executables/PDBs
are excluded from the portable whitelist. Successful freestanding firmware
ELF/RAM images are research data from the included own sources with
`-nostdlib -rtlib=none -fno-sanitize=all`; no OS/compiler runtime is embedded.
The failed pre-adaptation debug ELF is local-only and excluded from delivery.

This file records provenance and inclusion scope; it is not a public release.
