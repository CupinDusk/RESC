# Designed exact-family complete CPU jobs

Prospectively declared in R05_HOPPER_PROSPECTIVE_RESPONSE_PLAN.md before
inputs or observations. This is a deliberately favorable synthetic structural
study, not a representative application or population sample. It supplements
the unchanged adverse R04 timing evidence and never replaces it.

Fifteen complete rankings: n=4,8,16,32,64, with first adjacent pair, last
adjacent pair, or all disjoint adjacent pairs reversed from increasing order.
Forty-eight planted integer-log-unit rectangles per plan, exactly as specified
in the parent plan; all 720 distinct queries are kept. A job reuses Q=1,48,
192,768 of its fixed 48 queries. No query count adds distinct inputs.

Raw, Closed, Cheap, +Q use the same R04 functions and complete-job boundaries.
Only rectangle storage capacity changes 8 to 48 to hold the declared corpus;
the parser count, Q indexing and expected-hash arrays change accordingly.
Record an exact source diff. No algorithmic logic changes. No specialized
adjacent-reversal flag is added to the measured baseline. Such a flag could
recognize this known designed family more cheaply, so a gain cannot establish
the necessity or optimality of the general quartet test.

One untimed warmup then nine paired blocks. Seed 20261025 shuffles each
plan's four Q values and four route orders before observations. Fixed repeat
counts by n: 100,20,5,2,1 respectively. Each repeat performs a fresh complete
route-specific preparation. Common immutable input arrays/scratch capacity
are preloaded; all route setup, graph construction and rolling decision hash
are timed. Parsing, validation, output, expected checks and outer volatile
update are excluded equally. No affinity, priority, power or frequency edits.
All 2,160 timed records are retained from exactly one collection; no retiming
or choosing a favorable Q. Record performance only after complete Raw/Closed
BF, independent Floyd and edgewise certificates agree on all 720 queries,
and all 60 expected batch hashes agree across routes.

Report per-n/family/Q preparation-inclusive jobs, per-block paired ratios,
graph counts and no-gain outcomes. Do not pool sizes into an unlabeled median.
No GPU, outward log, certificate-service, cryptographic or physical time claim.
An independent agent must check structural exactness, all inputs, decisions,
hashes, raw records and full timing boundaries. Validation may be rerun;
performance collection must not be rerun to obtain a better result.
